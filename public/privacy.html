Privacy

The Manhattan Layout Editor runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own and no accounts, and it collects no analytics.

Your projects

Open projects are kept in your browser's own storage on your computer. Project files are only read or written when you choose to open or save one. A share link holds the project in the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to a server.

Google Drive (optional)

If you save a project to Google Drive, or open one from there, you sign in with Google and the editor asks for one permission: drive.appdata. With it, the editor can only use its own hidden folder in your Google Drive. It cannot see, read or change any of your other files.

In that folder the editor keeps the projects you saved there, so you can open them on another computer. This data goes straight from your browser to Google; it never passes through any other server. The sign-in token stays in your browser and runs out after an hour.

Without choosing Google Drive, the editor loads nothing from Google.

The editor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to save and open your projects, and is not shared with anyone, sold, or used for advertising.

Removing your data

Choose "Sign out of Google" in the editor's menu to sign out. To delete the projects saved in Google Drive, open Google Drive, go to Settings → Manage apps, and choose "Delete hidden app data" for this app. You can also revoke its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
